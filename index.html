WHO WE ARE

A corporation built for real people.

We believe EVE is better when players have room to learn, build, and play at their own pace. Whether you mine moons, manufacture ships, move freight, train for PvP, or simply relax with other pilots, you have a place here.

Our philosophy is simple: real life comes first. There are no mandatory fleets, attendance requirements, or activity quotas.

5%R4 moon tax, or equivalent compressed value

BLUEPRINT LIBRARY

Create copies from 420+ BPOs.

  1. Assemble and rename a Station Container.
  2. Place it in the Input/Output Hangar.
  3. Open Industry and select the BPO.
  4. Set your container as output.
  5. Choose “Owned by Corporation” and start the copy job.

BUYBACK PROGRAM

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions from new pilots.

Is participation mandatory?

No. Play when you can and participate in the activities you enjoy.

Do I have to use Buyback?

No. The program is optional, and you may sell your items anywhere.

Do I have to attend fleets?

No. There are no mandatory fleets or attendance requirements.

Can I borrow blueprints?

You can use the corporation library to create blueprint copies for your projects.

Can I ask beginner questions?

Absolutely. Helping new and returning players is one of our primary goals.
