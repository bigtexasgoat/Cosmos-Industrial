About the corporation

A place to learn, earn, and belong.

Founded on September 19, 2020, Cosmos Industrial operates primarily in high-security space as part of the Cosmos Origins alliance. Our focus is practical: help members develop reliable income streams, learn industry systems, and find people they enjoy flying with.

New players can ask beginner questions without judgment. Veterans can use our infrastructure, blueprint library, buyback program, and logistics network. Nullsec progression through Cosmos Collective is available, but never required.

“Fly safe, ask questions, and most importantly—have fun.”

Whether you mine, build, haul, PvP, or simply relax with fellow pilots, there is room for you here.

Moon mining and fleet activity

Members may mine moons owned by Cosmos Industrial, Cosmos Origins, Cosmos Collective, Cosmos Phoenix Reborn, and Cosmos Research.

Moon tax: 5% of mined moon ore, or equivalent compressed value, calculated using Janice. Taxes support structures, fuel, services, and events.

420+ Blueprint Originals

Corporation BPOs are available at Shedoo - Leo II. Members create copies rather than removing originals.

  1. Assemble a station or secure container in your hangar.
  2. Rename the container with your character name.
  3. Move it to the Input/Output hangar.
  4. In Industry, select the corporation BPO input and your container as output.
  5. Filter to corporation-owned blueprints, choose copy quantity, and start the job.
  6. Deliver the job when complete and retrieve copies from your container.

Frequently asked questions

Answers before you undock.

Is participation mandatory?

No. There are no mandatory fleets, attendance requirements, or activity quotas.

Do I have to use the buyback program?

No. It is completely optional.

Can I borrow blueprints?

Use corporation originals to create blueprint copies for your projects.

Can I ask beginner questions?

Absolutely. Helping new and returning pilots is one of the corporation's core goals.

Is nullsec required?

No. Stay in highsec as long as you like. Cosmos Collective is an optional progression path.

Who is friendly?

Corporation members are green, alliance members are dark blue, and friendly organizations are light blue. Agreements can vary by region, so ask before engaging.
