Discord Setup

Four simple steps

  1. Join the Discord.
    Use the permanent invite link provided by the corporation.
  2. Post your IGN.
    Introduce yourself in #public-chat using your main character name.
  3. Receive your roles.
    Leadership will assign the access roles needed for corporation channels.
  4. Start chatting.
    Ask questions, watch for fleets, and get to know the other pilots.
Remember: beginner questions are always welcome. Helping new and returning players is one of the corporation's main goals.
