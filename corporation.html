Cosmos Alliances

Overall:

  • The Nullsec and Highsec alliances are separate but still affected by each other in a more political overview perspective.
  • Since our Nullsec corp is part of the Goonswarm Federation alliance, there are certain things our highsec alliance must abide by as to not jeopardize that Nullsec alliance relationship.

Nullsec - Goonswarm Federation:

  • In Nullsec we are part of a much larger alliance run by Goonswarm Corporation.
  • This list is really longer and can be found in game in are curious, but the Cosmos Corp is:
