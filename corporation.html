About Us

Relaxed, useful, and player-focused.

Cosmos Industrial is a high-security corporation built around helping players make ISK, learn EVE, and enjoy the game with others.

Real Life Comes First: no mandatory fleets, attendance requirements, or activity quotas.

Corporation Rules

Respect the people behind the pilots.

Rule #1: Don't be a piece of garbage

Treat people with respect. There is zero tolerance for racism, sexism, homophobia, harassment, political arguments, or toxic behavior.

Rule #2: Real life comes first

Life always comes before EVE. Nobody will be punished for taking time away from the game.

Political Overview

The highsec and nullsec alliances are separate, but their actions can affect each other politically. Because the nullsec corporation is part of Goonswarm Federation, highsec members must avoid actions that could jeopardize that relationship.
