Moon Tax
  • 5% of mined moon ore (or equivalent compressed value), calculated from Janice
  • Taxes help fund corporation structures, fuel, services and events.

How to pay taxes:

  1. Open Corporation (Alt + E by default).
  2. Go to the Home tab.
  3. Click Donate ISK.
  4. Enter the amount of ISK you want to give.
  5. Click Donate.
