Moon Mining

Mine across the Cosmos network.

You may mine moons owned by Cosmos Industrial, Cosmos Origins, Cosmos Collective, Cosmos Phoenix Reborn, and Cosmos Research.

Moon Tax: 5% of mined moon ore, or the equivalent compressed value, calculated using Janice. Taxes fund structures, fuel, services, and events.

How to pay moon tax

  1. Open Corporation using Alt + E.
  2. Go to the Home tab.
  3. Click Donate ISK.
  4. Enter the amount and confirm.
