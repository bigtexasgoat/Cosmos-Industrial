Corporation Infrastructure

Structures

Cosmos Industrial primarily operates primarily out of Shedoo with the following structures:

Shedoo - Leo II - Raitaru - Manufacturing, Research & Invention:

Basic Medium Ship ME T1

Reduces materials needed to build Tech I medium ships (cruisers, mining barges, industrials, etc.).

Advanced Small Ship ME T1

Reduces materials needed to build advanced small ships, such as Tech II frigates, Tech II destroyers, Tech III destroyers, and other advanced small hulls.

Advanced Component ME T1

Reduces materials needed to build advanced components, primarily the components used in Tech II manufacturing (e.g., armor plates, reactor units, shield emitters, etc.).

Shedoo - Eridanis I - Athanor - Reprocessing:

Asteroid Ore T1

Increases reprocessing yield for all standard asteroid ores and their variants.

Veldspar, Scordite, Pyroxeres, Plagioclase, Omber, Kernite, Jaspet, Hemorphite, Hedbergite, Gneiss, Dark Ochre, Crokite, Bistot, Arkonor, Spodumain, and Mercoxit.

Ice T1

Increases reprocessing yield for all ice types

Blue Ice, Clear Icicle, Glacial Mass, White Glaze, Glare Crust, Gelidus, Krystallos, Dark Glitter and their variants.

Shedoo - Crux II - Astrahus - Clone Services:

Clone Bay

You guessed it, it allows you to clone your character!

Fera - Ashen Aspire I - Athanor - Reprocessing:

Moon Drilling Stability T1

improves the quality and flexibility of your moon extractions rather than increasing the amount of ore you get.

  • 20% smaller asteroid field radius – The moon belt spawns in a tighter cluster, so miners spend less time traveling between rocks.
  • 20% longer before automatic fracture – After the chunk reaches the extraction point, you have 20% more time to manually trigger the explosion before it self-fractures.
  • 50% longer asteroid lifetime – Once the moon chunk is fractured, the asteroids remain mineable for 50% longer before they despawn.
