FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers about participation, buyback, fleets, blueprints, standings, and nullsec.

Common Questions

Answers for new and returning pilots.

Is participation mandatory?

No. There are no mandatory fleets, activity quotas, or attendance requirements.

Do I have to use the buyback program?

No. Buyback is optional. You may keep, manufacture with, or sell your resources elsewhere.

Do I have to attend fleets?

No. Join fleets when they fit your schedule and interests.

Can I borrow blueprints?

You may use the corporation library to create blueprint copies. Corporation originals should remain in the library.

Can I ask beginner questions?

Absolutely. Helping new and returning players is one of the corporation's main goals.

Can I travel into nullsec beyond Shedoo?

Members of Cosmos Origins may explore the NPC Curse region, but should not shoot Imperium structures or travel beyond Curse and Great Wildlands without confirming current guidance. Political standings can change, so ask leadership before acting.

Who is considered Imperium?

The member guide lists Brave Collective, Dracarys, Fanatic Legion, Goonswarm Federation, Tactical Narcotics Team, Invidia Gloriae Comes, Scumlords, SONS of BANE, Stribog Clade, Sigma Grindset, and WE FORM BL0B. Always confirm the current in-game standings before engagement.

Is nullsec progression required?

No. Moving to Cosmos Collective is optional. You may remain in highsec as long as you want.
