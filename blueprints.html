Blueprint Library

Blueprint access requirements and the complete copying procedure.

  • Over 420 Blueprint Originals are available to make copies of at Station Leo II.
  • You can also find many blueprints already copied in the
  • To gain access to the BPO station hanger:
    • First you must be in the corp for a minimum of 1 week.
    • Then you can send Thiranoyama a DM on discord or message him in game to request access.
How to Use
  1. Get a station container or secure container and assemble it in your hangar.
  2. Rename the container with your name
  3. Drag and drop it in the Input/Ouput hangar
  4. Open the Blueprint tab in the Industry window
  5. Select the INPUT MATERIAL LOCATION as BPO
  6. Select the OUTPUT LOCATION to your container
  7. Select the first filter to Owned by corp
  8. Choose the number of copies you want to make
  9. Click start
  10. After it is done, click Done and it will go to your container.
  11. Enjoy your industry life
